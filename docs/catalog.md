# 🥗 Catalog & Dish Management

This section describes how to manage dishes, categories, and their hierarchical structure.

## 📁 Navigating the category tree
The main interface for browsing the product catalog is the **Tree View**. It lets you manage how dishes are organised across folders/categories.

![Catalog tree view](screenshots/catalog_tree.png)
*Figure 1: Hierarchical structure of the catalog.*

**How to access it:**
1.  Go to **Catalog → Products** in the sidebar.
2.  Pick the terminal you need (e.g. `origin`) from the **Select Ids** dropdown.
3.  A hierarchical tree appears, showing root folders such as `restoapp.org`, `Add-ons`, `Sides`, etc.

## 🖱️ Context menu (right-click actions)
To manage a specific folder or dish directly from the tree, use the right mouse button.

1.  **Open/View**: Right-click a folder or dish and choose **View** to inspect its contents or details.
2.  **Edit**: Choose **Edit** to change attributes such as SKU, name, prices, and descriptions.

![Product actions](screenshots/product_actions.png)
*Figure 2: Context menu for product actions (Edit, View, Delete).*

## 📝 Editing dishes and catalogs
Detailed product information can also be found under **Platform → Products**.

**Key attributes when editing:**
*   **SKU**: The technical unique identifier (article number).
*   **SEO metadata**: Title, keywords, and meta description for the website.
*   **Nutrition**: Proteins, fats, carbohydrates, and calories.
*   **Pricing**: Base and promotional prices.

For structural changes (such as adding new folders or moving products), the category tree is the preferred tool.
